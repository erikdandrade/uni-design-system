### Overview

Icon Buttons are compact, tappable elements that display only an icon—without text. They’re used for actions with recognizable symbols such as “edit,” “delete,” “close,” or “share.” They help conserve space and provide quick access to common actions.

### Principles

1. **Clarity:** Use universally understood icons that clearly communicate their purpose.

1. **Consistency:** Align icon size, color, and placement with other button styles to maintain visual harmony.

1. **Accessibility:** Always include accessible labels (`aria-label` or tooltips) describing the action.

1. **Focus State:** Ensure strong focus and hover states for accessibility and usability.
