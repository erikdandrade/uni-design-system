Link Button Overview  


### Overview

Link Buttons look like text links but behave like buttons, triggering in-page actions rather than navigation. They blend into content-heavy areas where a full button might feel too prominent.

### Principles

1. **Navigational:** Leads to another page, route, or external site.

1. **Action-based:** Triggers a local event such as expanding content or submitting lightweight actions.
