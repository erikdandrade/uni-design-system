### Primary Buttons

Use one primary button per context to help users do something specific, move forward in a flow, acknowledge and dismiss, or complete a task.

  
![Do](/images/b8f03047-f1b1-4dcf-99c4-fc255c158cf9.png)  
Do, Use one primary button per context to highlight the action the user will most likely take.  
  
![Don't](/images/9a12b68e-9862-4428-902c-bc33acd857da.png)  
Don't, Avoid using multiple primary buttons per panel or inside an element.  
  


### Secondary Buttons

Secondary buttons are the cornerstone of button functionality. Embrace their usage whenever feasible. Their white background and gray border enhance visibility, making them more accessible compared to tertiary buttons.

  
![Secondary Button](/images/9bc827be-db1b-469a-a31c-82a4ad6ecbd1.png)  
Secondary Button  


### Tertiary Buttons

Tertiary buttons primarily serve for actions that allow users to dismiss or skip something, providing an exit option. They offer users the choice to take no action or dismiss a prompt.
