## Overview

Typography plays a critical role in establishing a clear and consistent visual hierarchy across all digital products. In the UNI Design System, we have developed a flexible yet structured typography system that ensures readability, accessibility, and brand alignment. This section outlines the typography guidelines and best practices that contribute to a cohesive and user-friendly experience across all touchpoints.


## Typeface

UNI uses a primary typeface that reflects the brand's voice and tone while supporting various digital platforms. Our chosen typeface is **Inter**, selected for its versatility, legibility, and modern aesthetic.

- **Font Family:** Inter

- **Weight Variants:** Regular, Medium, Semibold, Bold

### Typeface Usage

- **Headlines (H1–H5):** Use bold or medium weights for hierarchy and emphasis.

- **Body Text:** Regular weight is used for general content, providing readability at various sizes.

- **Emphasis Text:** Use italics or bold sparingly for emphasis within the body copy.

- **Monospace Font (if applicable):** For code snippets or technical elements, we use **JetBrains Mono**.

## Color Contrast & Accessibility

Ensuring sufficient color contrast is essential for making text accessible to all users, including those with visual impairments. The UNI typography system follows the WCAG (Web Content Accessibility Guidelines) 2.1 standards for contrast ratios:

- **Minimum contrast ratio:** 4.5:1 for regular text.

- **For large text (18px bold or 24px regular):** A minimum contrast ratio of 3:1.

To achieve this, our type is paired with colors from the UNI palette, ensuring all text meets or exceeds accessibility guidelines.

## Spacing

Consistency in text alignment and spacing is crucial to maintaining a clean, organized layout. To ensure that, we maintain a space of at least **1.4x** the line height between paragraphs for a balanced layout.

## Typographic Scale & Modular System

UNI utilizes a **modular scale** to maintain proportionality across all text sizes, ensuring a harmonious and balanced hierarchy. We use a **1.15** or **1.5** scale, depending on the context of the design.

## Best Practices

To maintain high standards across the board, the following best practices should be adhered to:

- **Clarity and Simplicity:** Prioritize readability over decorative text. 

- **Consistency:** Use predefined typography styles to create uniformity across pages and products.

- **Legibility on All Devices:** Test font sizes and line heights across devices to ensure text is easily readable without zooming or straining.

- **Avoid Text as Images:** Use actual text for any copy that needs to be accessible and SEO-friendly, rather than embedding text in images.

## Language Support

UNI’s typography system is designed to support multiple languages and writing systems, ensuring that it scales and adjusts appropriately for different character sets. This includes full support for:

- **Latin scripts** (English, Spanish, French, etc.)

- **Non-Latin scripts** (Arabic, Chinese, Cyrillic, etc.)

- Base Font Size: 14px

- Multiplier: 1.15 (Rounded to closest value)

- Scaled sizes: 16px, 18px
