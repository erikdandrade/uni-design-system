# Introduction

The Foundations of the UNI Design System provide the essential building blocks that define our visual language and user experience principles. These foundational elements—such as color, typography, spacing, and iconography—serve as the backbone of every design decision, ensuring a consistent and cohesive experience across all products.

By establishing a shared understanding of these core elements, the Foundations section creates a unified approach to design that promotes clarity, accessibility, and brand integrity. Each aspect has been meticulously crafted to maintain balance between aesthetic appeal and functional usability, empowering teams to create visually engaging and user-friendly interfaces.

Whether you're starting a new project or refining an existing one, the Foundations section is your guide to maintaining consistency and ensuring that every design adheres to the UNI system's standards of quality and excellence.
