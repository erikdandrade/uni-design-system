### Overview

Banners provide contextual or important information at the page or section level and may be dismissible or persistent.

### Principles

1. **Hierarchy:** Banners should inform without overpowering primary content.

1. **Context: **Information should be relevant to the current page or user task.

1. **Persistence:** Critical information should remain visible until resolved or dismissed.
