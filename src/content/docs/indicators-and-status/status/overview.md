### Overview

Use Status components to **communicate the current state of an item at a glance**, most commonly in data-dense interfaces like tables, lists, or dashboards, where quick scanning is essential.

### Principles

1. **Scannability:** Status should be easily recognizable at a glance, especially in tables.

1. **Consistency:** The same status must always use the same label, color, and icon.

1. **Clarity:** Text and optional icons should clearly convey the state.

1. **Accessibility:** Status meaning should not rely on color alone.
