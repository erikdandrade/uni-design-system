### Overview

**Breadcrumbs** are a secondary navigational aid that show a user’s current location within a site or application hierarchy. They present a trail—typically a series of clickable links—that illustrates the path from the home (or starting) page to the current page, enabling users to easily backtrack to higher levels.

### Principles

1. **Hierarchy Clarity: **Breadcrumbs must accurately reflect the underlying site structure so users can easily understand where they are.

1. **Simplicity: **Keep the breadcrumb trail concise and focused. Separate levels with clear delimiters (like “`uni-icon` with chevron icon”).

1. **Interactivity: **All items (except the current page) should be clickable, allowing users to navigate back effortlessly.

1. **Responsiveness: **Adapt breadcrumb display for various screen sizes—use truncation or ellipsis on mobile devices when needed.
