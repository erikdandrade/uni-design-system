### Overview

The Tabs component is a user interface element designed primarily for navigation purposes. It allows users to switch between different sections or views within the platform, providing an organized and intuitive way to navigate through related content.

### Principles

1. **Navigation**: The primary purpose of the Tabs component is to facilitate navigation between different sections or views of the platform.

1. **Multiple Views**: Each tab represents a distinct section or view of the content, allowing users to easily switch between them.

1. **Content Area**: Below the Tabs component, there is typically a content area that displays the content associated with the currently selected tab.

1. **Persistent State**: The selected tab and its associated content often remain visible or highlighted, providing users with a clear indication of their current location within the platform.
