### Overview

Separators are 1px horizontal or vertical lines used to visually divide content and improve layout clarity.

### Principles

1. **Purpose:** Separators should clarify content structure, not decorate.

1. **Subtlety: **Visual weight should remain minimal and unobtrusive.
