Button Group Overview  


### Overview

A Button Group is a horizontal or vertical collection of buttons that act as related controls. It visually connects multiple options and indicates grouping logic — for example, alignment tools, filters, or toggle states.

### Principles

1. **Logical Grouping:** Combine only related or mutually exclusive actions.

1. **Visual Cohesion: **Buttons share the same size and variant.
