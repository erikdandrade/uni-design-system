### Overview Testing Here

The button component is a fundamental UI element that allows users to trigger actions or navigate to different sections within an application. It provides a consistent and visually appealing way to interact with the interface.

### Principles

1. **Recognizable**: Buttons should clearly show that they can do something when clicked.

1. **Locatable**: They should stand out and be easy to spot, even among other elements like other buttons.

1. **Transparent**: Their purpose and current status should be easily understood.

### Hierarchy

Button hierarchy highlights the relative importance of buttons within a context, enabling users to prioritize actions accordingly. Buttons use color and contrast to create three levels of emphasis and hierarchy. The buttons with the strongest emphasis are primary, followed by secondary, then tertiary. 

  
![Primary](https://studio-assets.supernova.io/design-systems/145982/6240b6c6-e6cf-41ad-8e3c-0621b55ddcf2.png)  
Primary, Used for the main call-to-action or the most important action on the page.  
  
![Secondary](https://studio-assets.supernova.io/design-systems/145982/17278483-55b3-41a4-9aa6-76e980982b23.png)  
Secondary, Used for secondary actions or complementary actions to the primary button.  
  
![Tertiary](https://studio-assets.supernova.io/design-systems/145982/109ba66f-4450-4bf4-bdbd-105ef435c7f3.png)  
Tertiary, Used for less important actions or alternative options.

### Destructive Buttons

Destructive buttons initiate actions resulting in irreversible data loss. Due to their non&hyphen;reversible nature, establishing button hierarchy is crucial to minimize errors.
