Compact Button Overview  


### Overview

The Compact Button is a **minimal, text&hyphen;based control** used in dense layouts (like tables, lists, or inline text). It removes background and padding to reduce visual clutter while still behaving like a standard button.

### Principles

1. **Compactness:** Designed for data&hyphen;dense environments; prioritize function over decoration.

1. **Minimal Visual Weight:** Appears text&hyphen;like but offers clear hover and focus feedback.
