
# Usage

---

### Main Usage

The Segmented Tabs component is commonly used when content needs to be organized and filtered based on distinct categories or criteria, such as product listings, properties filtration, or data visualization dashboards.

  
![Do](https://studio-assets.supernova.io/design-systems/145982/504c4784-cac3-48be-a114-1f566410c083.png)  
Do, Use concise and descriptive labels for each cell, making it obvious to users what content or filter they represent.  
  
![Don't](https://studio-assets.supernova.io/design-systems/145982/a2d77a47-8cf5-4978-92b0-f8f1884abadf.png)  
Don't, Avoid using ambiguous or confusing labels for the tabs, as this can lead to user confusion and frustration.  
  


  
![Do](https://studio-assets.supernova.io/design-systems/145982/c7daa878-aef7-4beb-984f-120d89fde98b.png)  
Do, Try using a minimum of 2 and a maximum of 5 to maintain a clean, organized appearance.  
  
![Don't](https://studio-assets.supernova.io/design-systems/145982/60586b05-159f-4618-b6bb-619b9f6499c0.png)  
Don't, Limit the number of tabs or segments to prevent overcrowding and maintain a clean, organized appearance.  
  


  
![Do](https://studio-assets.supernova.io/design-systems/145982/a47221b1-2da4-42ae-b362-614c4f9094a4.png)  
Do, Group related content or sections together under a single tab, following a logical structure and hierarchy.  
  
![Don't](https://studio-assets.supernova.io/design-systems/145982/77437294-c005-4104-91a3-5b0fc6f0f3eb.png)  
Don't, Group unrelated content or sections together under a single tab to prevent confusion and frustration.
