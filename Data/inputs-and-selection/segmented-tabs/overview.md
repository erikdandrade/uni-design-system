Segmented Tabs Overview  


### Overview

The Segmented Tabs component is a user interface element designed mainly for filtering and categorizing content. It typically consists of a horizontal bar or row with multiple tabs or segments, each representing a specific filter or category. When a tab is clicked or selected, the corresponding filter is applied to the content displayed below or within the component.

### Principles

1. **Filtering**: The primary purpose of the Segmented Tabs component is to filter and display specific subsets of content based on the selected tab or category.

1. **Mutually Exclusive**: Only one tab can be active or selected at a time, ensuring a clear separation between different categories or filters.

1. **Visual Distinction**: The active tab is visually distinguished from the inactive tabs, often through color changes, borders, or other visual cues, making it easy for users to identify the currently applied filter.

1. **Content Area**: Below or within the Segmented Tabs component, there is typically a content area that dynamically updates to display the filtered or categorized content based on the selected tab
