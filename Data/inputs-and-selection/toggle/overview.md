### Overview

A toggle is a user interface control that allows users to switch between two mutually exclusive states or options. Toggles are commonly used for enabling or disabling specific features or settings, or for toggling between different modes or views.

### Principles

1. **Binary State**: Toggles represent an on/off or true/false state.

1. **Immediate Feedback**: Provide visual feedback on toggle state changes and don’t require confirmation like checkboxes.

1. **No Actions**: Switches immediately activate a state, not perform a single action.
