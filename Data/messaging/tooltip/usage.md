### Main Usage

The **Tooltip** component provides contextual information to enhance user interactions without overwhelming the interface. It should be used selectively and thoughtfully to support users by offering brief, supplementary explanations where necessary.

![Do](https://studio-assets.supernova.io/design-systems/145982/a39f8df2-83b2-427e-aef9-a9e154e5890b.png)  
Keep content brief and to the point  
Don't  
![Don't](https://studio-assets.supernova.io/design-systems/145982/20c499b7-4bd8-4d39-8650-3e7a0b197328.png)  
Include lengthy paragraphs or complex information
