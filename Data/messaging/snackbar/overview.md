Snackbar Overview  


### Overview

Snackbars provide **immediate, non&hyphen;blocking system feedback** in response to user actions. They are meant to confirm outcomes, surface errors, or share brief updates without interrupting the user’s workflow. 

### Principles

1. **Clarity:** Messages should clearly communicate the outcome of an action in a single, concise sentence.

1. **Timeliness:** Snackbars should appear immediately after the triggering action.

1. **Non&hyphen;intrusiveness:** Feedback should not block user interaction or interrupt the workflow.

1. **Dismissibility:** Persistent snackbars should always allow manual dismissal.
