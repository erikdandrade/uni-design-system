## Overview

Design tokens are a key component of a scalable and flexible design system. They allow design decisions to be abstracted into a systematized structure, enabling consistency across different platforms and improving the handoff between design and development. In the UNI Design System, design tokens are used to manage and standardize UI properties such as color, typography, spacing, and more, ensuring that the product remains cohesive, adaptable, and maintainable.

## Best Practices

1. Always use token variables instead of hard&hyphen;coded values

1. Maintain a clear naming convention for easy understanding and scalability

1. Document any custom tokens or overrides for team reference

1. Regularly review and update tokens to keep the design system current
